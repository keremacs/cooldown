What is Cooldown?

A small desktop app that understands when you're getting tired at the computer — and gently reminds you when it's time for a break.

What does it do?

Cooldown is built for developers and anyone who spends long hours at a computer. While you keep working, it quietly watches in the background — tracking errors, how often you switch apps, and how intensely you're working. Then it gives you a Fatigue Score from 0 to 100.

As the score rises, it suggests taking a break. The goal isn't to push you harder — it's to help you stop before you're completely exhausted.

How does it work? (4 steps)

  • 1
    It quietly watches your work It notices coding errors, failed terminal commands, and which apps you spend time in. You don't need to do anything extra.
  • 2
    It calculates your fatigue score It combines the signals into a live score. A low score means you're doing fine; as it climbs, fatigue is building.
  • 3
    It keeps data only on your computer Nothing is sent to the internet. No cloud uploads, no accounts. Everything stays on your device.
  • 4
    It nudges you when needed A small icon or notification says "A break would help." You can snooze it if you're in the zone.
🟢 You're doing great 0 – 40
🟡 Getting tired 40 – 75
🔴 Break time 75 – 100

What is it good for?

  • 📊
    Shows your fatigue score See how tired you are right now at a glance.
  • 💻
    Connects to your code editor Automatically detects errors in VS Code and Cursor.
  • ⌨️
    Listens to your terminal Counts failed commands in PowerShell or Zsh.
  • 🔒
    Respects your privacy Data stays on your computer. Nothing is shared.
  • ☕
    Reminds you to take breaks Alerts you when you've been going too long without resting.
  • 📱
    Runs in the background Lives in your menu bar or system tray. Lightweight and unobtrusive.

Who is it for?

Built primarily for software developers — but anyone who sits at a computer all day can benefit: students, designers, writers, remote workers… If you ever think "I didn't realize how tired I was until it was too late," Cooldown is for you.

In short

Cooldown is a helper app that listens while you work, gently warns you when fatigue builds up, and reminds you to rest. Easy to install, works offline, and your data belongs to you.

Less burnout. Smarter breaks. Healthier work.
